## Featured Project

### [Jeevan Mitra](https://github.com/manojga56565/Jeevan-Mitra)

**A Coordination Layer Connecting Hospitals, Blood Banks, And Potentially Eligible Blood Donors.**

Jeevan Mitra Is My Main Project And The Idea I’m Committed To Developing Further.

The Problem It Addresses Is Not Simply A Lack Of Donors. During Urgent Blood Requirements, The Right Information And People Are Not Always Connected Quickly. Jeevan Mitra Explores How Better Coordination Can Help.

**The Proposed Workflow**

Hospital Requirement → Donor Matching → Relevant Alerts → Donation Coordination → Verification

**Core Project Areas**

- Blood Requirement Coordination
- Donor Discovery By Blood Group And Location
- Eligibility And Donation Cooldown Checks
- Donor QR Identification And Hospital Side Verification
- Request Tracking And Coordination

**An Important Boundary**

Jeevan Mitra Is Not A Blood Bank And Does Not Replace Medical Professionals. Donor Screening, Compatibility Testing, Blood Collection, Storage, And Transfusion Decisions Must Remain With Authorized Blood Banks And Healthcare Professionals.

**Development Status**

This Is An Active Work In Progress. Recent Repository Updates Include Work On The Frontend PWA Foundation, Public Statistics, District Data, Donor QR Access, And Hospital Side QR Verification Integration. These Areas Still Require Deployment And End To End Testing. I Will Mark Features As Complete Only After They Have Been Verified.
